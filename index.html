About T4n OS

T4n OS is a GNU/Linux based on Void Linux, designed with minimalism, speed, and lightness in mind. It uses runit as its init system to provide maximum performance and full system control.

T4n OS was developed in Indonesia, Atjeh, and was first initiated by Gh0sT4n on August 17, 2025, a student from Aceh (Darussa'adah Cot Tarom Baroh - SMKN 5 Takengon). The development of T4n OS uses a community-driven approach: from the community, by the community, and for the community.

In addition, T4n OS adopts a repository concept inspired by AUR (Arch User Repository), namely VUR (Void User Repository), to make package distribution and management easier for users.
